# Incremental selection

A short paper on the operator P_k, which extracts the k smallest elements of a list, and on computing it block by block.

- `source/`: the manuscript as first submitted, and copies of references that are hard to find, brought in by hand.
- `artifacts/`: the revised version of the paper, and the detailed proofs.
- `mechanization/`: Lean definitions and theorems; `mechanization/CORRESPONDENCE.md` matches the paper's results to Lean names.
